# SEAM REPLICATION — is the period texture actually seamless?
#
# The tiled view only holds together if whatever overhangs one edge of the world is ALSO drawn at the
# opposite edge, so neighbouring copies line up. Without that, cells on the boundary get sliced off.
#
# At `camera zoom 0.5` the view is 1.5 periods tall and 2 periods wide, so with the camera at the origin the
# world's +x edge sits at screen u=0.75 and its +y edge at v=0.1667. Cells are spawned exactly there:
#
#   (0.75, 0.1667) -> world (+HALF, +HALF), the CORNER: needs all four images
#   (0.75, 0.5)    -> world (+HALF, 0),     the +x edge: needs two
#   (0.5,  0.1667) -> world (0, +HALF),     the +y edge: needs two
#
# In tile-seam-on.png each of those must appear whole on both/all sides, not cut in half.

campaign ch00-genesis
runs 2
night 1
matterlayer all

camera zoom 0.5
spawn 0.75 0.1667
spawn 0.75 0.5
spawn 0.5 0.1667
runs 2

tile off
shot tile-seam-off

tile on
shot tile-seam-on
