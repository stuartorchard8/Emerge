# The move tool: press on a machine, carry it, turn it, let go.
#
# `PLAN_machine_relocation.md` increment 2. What is worth photographing is the middle of the
# gesture — the cursor holding a real machine — because that is the only part of this tool that
# draws anything, and it is drawn through the same placement cursor a fresh build uses.
#
# What to read in the shots:
#   `move-tool`     -- the tool taken out with nothing in hand. The panel describes a GESTURE
#                      rather than a target, because this is the one tool that acts on a release.
#   `carrying`      -- a concentrator held over clear deck, in the fits colour, with its ports
#                      drawn on the sides they will actually be on. It is still standing where it
#                      was: nothing is edited until the button comes up.
#   `carrying-turned` -- R, and the same machine held a quarter turn round. Read the ports.
#   `carrying-refused` -- held over the other machine, in the refusal colour. Letting go here is
#                      the cancel: the machine goes back where it was.
#   `moved`         -- let go somewhere it fits. One edit, and it took its casing, its stores and
#                      its heat with it.

new
# ⚠️ **Creative, because a freshly placed machine is a GHOST** and a ghost does not move — which the
# tool is right to refuse and which makes it useless as a subject. These two arrive built.
creative 1
camera centre 27 10 36

brush CONCENTRATOR Right
material Iron
place 24 12
place 30 12
run 4

reach MOVE
shot move-tool

# ── Press on it, and carry it over clear deck ────────────────────────────────
take 24 12
carry 24 8
shot carrying

# ── R turns what is being held ───────────────────────────────────────────────
carry 24 8 Down
shot carrying-turned

# ── Held over the other machine: refused, and letting go here cancels ────────
carry 30 12 Right
shot carrying-refused

# ── Let go somewhere it fits ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
carry 24 8 Right
drop
run 4
shot moved
