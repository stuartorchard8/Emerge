# The circuit overlay: what is wired to what, and what is moving through it.
#
# `PLAN_power_network.md` increment 3b. Two readings in one view, because a short is silent in
# either alone -- the conductor is TINTED BY CIRCUIT, which works on a dead ship, and carriers are
# ANIMATED ALONG IT in the solved direction, which works on a live one.
#
# What to read in the shots:
#   `circuit-plain` -- the panel and its loop of cable, with no overlay on;
#   `circuit-tint`  -- the loop wearing ONE colour, because it is one circuit; a rail crossing it
#                      keeps its own, because charge crosses layers only where a terminal stands;
#   `circuit-live`  -- carriers streaming along the loop. ⚠️ They run against the conventional
#                      current, because carriers are ELECTRONS.
#
# ⚠️ The panel is FORSTERITE. A conductive casing is a parallel path between a machine's own two
# terminals, so a steel panel shorts itself and drives nothing -- `PLAN_power_network.md` §5.
