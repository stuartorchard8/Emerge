# The fabric and the air, coupled: a furnace should heat itself, then its room, then drive a
# circulation without anything being told to circulate.
#
# What to read:
#   `temp`    is the hottest *solid* on each tile — the furnace should stand out sharply, because
#             firebrick conducts badly and is meant to hold its own heat.
#   `airtemp` should show a warm plume ABOVE the furnace rather than a symmetric blob around it.
#             That asymmetry is the whole result: nothing in the heat pass knows which way is up, so
#             an updraught can only come from `P = nT` making warm gas light and buoyancy lifting it.
#   both balances must stay 0. Coupling joined two previously independent ledgers, and the one
#   failure mode that matters is heat being minted at the join rather than moved across it.
new
# Feedstock first. Since H3 the plant makes nothing until something is put on the extractor's plate,
# so a script about a furnace at work has to say where the ore is coming from — see §5i.
rock 5 12
run 400

echo --- the fabric, after four hundred ticks of smelting
field temp

echo --- and the air it is heating
field airtemp

echo --- at the furnace, and one tile above it
probe 20 12
probe 20 10

echo --- the ledgers
expect heatBalance = 0
expect airHeatBalance = 0
expect hottestSolidK > 350
expect hottestAirK > 300

overlay HEAT
camera centre 20 12 26
shot heat-fabric
