# The water injector, and the phase machinery it exists to let you poke at.
#
# There is no other way to get a liquid into this world, so without this tool the entire equation
# of state can only be exercised from tests. What to read:
#
#   `airBalance` must stay 0 throughout. The injector MINTS matter, which is the one thing the
#   ledgers exist to forbid, so it mints it and admits it — `injectedAir` is the admission and the
#   balance is the check that the admission is complete.
#
#   `species:Water` shows where the water is. Held for 64 ticks it should build a tile up toward a
#   full pool rather than immediately spreading everywhere.
#
# ⚠️ The water arrives at 230K, not room temperature, and that is a known error being worked
# around rather than a design choice: van der Waals carries no acentric factor, so this model puts
# water's boiling point at one atmosphere near -33C. See PLAN_phase_transitions.md §5c.
new 0

echo --- a dry room
expect injectedAir = 0

# Half a tile's worth, into the middle of the deck.
water 8 12 32
echo --- after 32 ticks of pouring
probe 8 12
field species:Water 4 9 12 15
expect airBalance = 0

run 60
echo --- and sixty ticks later: how far has it spread, and is any of it still a pool?
field species:Water 4 9 12 15
expect airBalance = 0

shot water
