# The cut tool. Severs joins without taking anything up -- the exact opposite of the build gesture.
#
# Conduit connects by being DRAWN, never by touching, so a run is only a run where the player
# dragged one. Until this existed the only way to break a line was to delete a tile of it and lay it
# again, which meant paying for the tile twice and, outside creative, waiting for a ghost to build
# itself back.
#
# What to read in the shots:
#   `before` -- one unbroken run east of the tank, carrying;
#   `after`  -- the same track, unchanged in colour and mass, with nothing moving past x=38.
#
# ⚠️ It cuts every join of every tile the stroke passes over, not merely the joins running along the
# stroke. A stroke ACROSS a belt is the gesture it exists for, and one that only cut parallel edges
# would do nothing at all in exactly that case.

new
creative 1
camera centre 36 9 14

brush RAIL
drag 32 9 39 9
run 60
shot cut-before

# A stroke across the belt, not along it.
cut 36 8 36 10 RAIL
run 60
shot cut-after

probe 36 9
probe 35 9

tool CUT RAIL
shot cut-panel
