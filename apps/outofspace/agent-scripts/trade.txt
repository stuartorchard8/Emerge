# The counter. What a berth will pay for what you are carrying, and what it will sell you.
#
# The trade sheet opens itself the moment the clamps take -- a berth is somewhere the player flew to
# on purpose, so putting the counter in front of them answers the question they arrived with. It can
# be closed, and the way back is the docking port's own panel, which is what makes closing it safe.
#
# What to read in the shots:
#   `berthed`   -- the sheet as it opens itself: balance, berth, and the two lists;
#   `listed`    -- one species put up for sale; its row goes green and the network starts routing;
#   `ordered`   -- one species on order; the same green, on the other list;
#   `closed`    -- the sheet dismissed, the world back;
#   `reopened`  -- the way back in, from the docking port's BERTH section;
#   `narrow`    -- the same counter on a phone-shaped screen, where the sheet comes off the bottom;
#   `reloaded`  -- a world SAVED while berthed, loaded back: the counter stays shut, because a load
#                  is not an arrival, and the berth behind it still has a counterparty.
#
# ⚠️ Two columns because they answer two questions. SELL is what is LOOSE aboard -- the same view the
# material picker reads -- because the only thing worth offering is something the network can deliver
# to the mouth. BUY is what the station is holding, because that is the whole of what is on offer.
#
# ⚠️ Prices are per hundred kilograms and there are two of them: the bid on the sell side and the ask
# on the buy side. They differ, and one "price" column would be lying about one of them.

new
creative 1
tool build
camera centre 44 9 14

# A docking port out on the pad, facing starboard, and a station brought to meet its mouth. The
# camera is pulled back far enough to hold the ship AND the twenty tiles of station beside it.
brush DockingPort Right
material STEEL
place 38 9
berth 38 9
shot berthed

# Put the ship's iron up for sale. The row goes green: from here the network routes iron to the mouth.
tap IRON
shot listed

# And order some titanium off the shelf.
tap TITANIUM
shot ordered

# Close it. A counter has to be closable or a berth is a modal prison.
tap close
shot closed

# The way back: the docking port's own panel carries the door. `berth` is the section that says
# where you are clamped, offers the interlock, and holds the way back to the counter.
inspect 38 9
shot berthpanel

tap TRADE
shot reopened

# ⛔ **A save made berthed comes back berthed, and the counter does not open itself.**
#
# Two things go wrong here if they are not held apart. The sheet opens on the TRANSITION into a
# berth, and across a load the frame before said "not berthed" -- so every docked save used to come
# up with the counter already over the ship. And the market is the station's, not the vessel's, so it
# is not written to the file: a load that did not go and find it again left the clamps shut with
# nobody behind them, the sheet reading "not berthed", and the mouth trading nothing ever again.
tap close
save agent-out/berthed.save
load agent-out/berthed.save
run 2
shot reloaded
inspect 38 9
tap TRADE
shot reloaded-counter
