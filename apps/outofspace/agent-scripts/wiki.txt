# every species named in the article is a way into the next one.

# and then RUTILE in titanium's own reactions — which is the whole navigation, and it can only pass

# (four reactions); its head (name, back/forward, CLOSE) is pinned and its body scrolls, so
# `wiki-carbon-scrolled` must still show the name and the way out while showing MADE BY.
#

# below the fold until `scroll` has brought it up. That is the player's position too.
