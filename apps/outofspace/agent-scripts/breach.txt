# A hull breach, which is the fluid sim's loudest behaviour and therefore the best thing to point a
# new instrument at: cut one hull tile and the room should empty through it, fast, in one direction.
#
# What to read: `flow` should show a coherent jet out of the hole rather than noise, `speed` should
# peak AT the hole, and airBalance must stay 0 — every gram that leaves is vented, not lost.
new
remove concentrator concentrator-5
run 1
echo --- immediately after the cut
field flow
probe concentrator concentrator-5

echo --- and as the room empties
trend 6 10
field speed
field flow
field pressure

# `airVented` counts air leaving the *grid*, not air leaving the hull — the void tiles around the
# vessel are still part of the world, so the two are different things and the harness was how that
# got noticed. This used to assert `airVented = 0`, on the reasoning that the plume would still be
# crossing the void by the time the script ended.
#
# It is now > 0, and the change is the point. That assertion was quietly encoding a bug: before
# `applyPressureForce` existed the gas was not slow to reach the rim, it was barely moving at all —
# a breached vessel measured `dAir = 0` for two hundred straight ticks. Nothing propagated the news
# of the hole, so nothing left. Air that actually blows out reaches the rim well inside this run.
expect airBalance = 0
expect airVented > 0
expect peakSpeed > 0

overlay FLOW
camera centre 20 12 26
shot breach-flow
overlay PRESSURE
shot breach-pressure
