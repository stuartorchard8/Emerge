# A hull breach, which is the fluid sim's loudest behaviour and therefore the best thing to point a
# new instrument at: cut one hull tile and the room should empty through it, fast, in one direction.
#
# What to read: `flow` should show a coherent jet out of the hole rather than noise, `speed` should
# peak AT the hole, and airBalance must stay 0 — every gram that leaves is vented, not lost.
new
remove 20 7
run 1
echo --- immediately after the cut
field flow
probe 20 7

echo --- and as the room empties
trend 6 10
field speed
field flow
field pressure

# Note what this does NOT assert: `airVented` stays 0, and correctly so — that counter is air leaving
# the *grid*, and this breach empties into the void tiles around the hull, which are still part of the
# world. "Vented" and "outside the hull" are different things, and the harness was how that got
# noticed. What must hold is conservation, and that the air is actually moving.
expect airBalance = 0
expect airVented = 0
expect peakSpeed > 0

overlay FLOW
camera centre 20 12 26
shot breach-flow
overlay PRESSURE
shot breach-pressure
