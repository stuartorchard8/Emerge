# Flight controls: a thruster answers the pilot's keys, and says so in its own panel.
#
# What to read in the shots:
#   `thruster-idle`     -- CONTROL section, LISTENS TO FLIGHT, PUSHES LEFT, FIRING 0%.
#                          Nobody is holding anything, so the motor is shut. This is the behaviour
#                          that replaced "always on".
#   `thruster-firing`   -- the same panel with LEFT held: FIRING 100%. Nothing was wired, nothing
#                          was bound to a key, and the motor worked out for itself that a push to
#                          port is what was asked for.
#   `thruster-wired`    -- switched over to WIRE, where it is a machine again and its WIRING drives
#                          it. The stick no longer reaches it.
new
creative 1
camera centre 34 9 64

# In the starboard wall, exhausting outboard: it pushes the ship to port.
brush THRUSTER RIGHT
place 34 9

tool INSPECT
inspect 34 9 DECK
shot thruster-idle

hold LEFT
inspect 34 9 DECK
shot thruster-firing

# Asking for the opposite: a motor that would fight the request stays shut.
hold RIGHT
inspect 34 9 DECK
shot thruster-opposed

# And the switch back to the wire, where it is a machine again: the stick no longer reaches it and
# its own WIRING drives it.
release
tap LISTENS TO
inspect 34 9 DECK
shot thruster-wired
